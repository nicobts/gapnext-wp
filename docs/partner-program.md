## How the Technology Works (Non-Technical Summary)

1. **Your consultant completes the audit checklist** — using the GapNext WordPress plugin or your existing workflow
2. **They click "Generate Report"** — the checklist data is sent to the AI pipeline
3. **In 30–60 seconds**, a complete, structured GAP analysis report is returned — findings, risk ratings, non-conformities, recommendations, and conclusions
4. **If corrections are needed**, the consultant adds notes and regenerates — the AI incorporates the feedback
5. **Download the PDF** and deliver it to the client under your brand

The AI is trained on compliance language and ISO standard requirements. It writes like a professional auditor, not a generic language model.
