# Gap Analysis Checklist UNI CEI EN ISO 13485:2016

> Checklist based on the **ISO 13485:2016** standard — Quality management systems for medical devices.  
> Includes **100 control points** based on the main text of the standard (Clauses 4–8).

---

## Methodology

- **Response Options:** 1) Compliant, 2) Partially Compliant, 3) Non-Compliant, 4) Not Applicable
- **Notes Field:** Mandatory for describing evidence or reasons for non-applicability
- **Attachments:** Field for uploading supporting files (e.g., procedures, risk plans, validations)
