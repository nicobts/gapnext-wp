# Integrated Gap Analysis Checklist ISO/IEC 42001:2023

> Checklist based on the **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** standard — Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS).  
> Includes **100 control points**: management system requirements (Clauses 4–10) + Annex A controls + Annex B operational guidelines.

---

## Methodology

- **Consecutive Numbering:** 1–100
- **Response Options:** 1) Compliant, 2) Partially Compliant, 3) Non-Compliant, 4) Not Applicable
- **Mandatory Fields:** Descriptive notes for evidence and attachment upload field
