# Gap Analysis Checklist ISO/IEC 27001:2022

> Checklist based on the **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** standard (Information Security Management System – ISMS).  
> Includes **179 control points**: 86 management system requirements (Clauses 4–10) + 93 Annex A controls.

---

## Checklist Methodology

- **Consecutive Numbering:** 1–179
- **Response Options:**
  1. Compliant
  2. Partially Compliant
  3. Non-Compliant
  4. Not Applicable
- **Mandatory Fields:** Descriptive notes (objective evidence) and attachment upload
