# Gap Analysis Checklist ISO 22163:2023

> Checklist based on the **ISO 22163:2023** standard (Railway applications — Railway quality management system — ISO 9001:2015 with particular requirements for the railway sector / evolution of the IRIS standard).  
> Includes **100 control points** structured according to the ISO 9001 High Level Structure.

---

## Methodology

- **Response Options:** 1) Compliant, 2) Partially Compliant, 3) Non-Compliant, 4) Not Applicable
- **Notes:** Mandatory field for describing objective evidence
- **Attachments:** Field for uploading procedures, KPIs, project plans, etc.
