# Gap Analysis Checklist UNI EN 9100:2018

> Checklist based on the **UNI EN 9100:2018** standard, specific to aerospace, space, and defense organizations.  
> Includes **100 control points** based on normative requirements, highlighting supplementary aerospace sector requirements.

---

## Checklist Methodology

- **Consecutive Numbering:** 1–100
- **Response Options:**
  1. Compliant
  2. Partially Compliant
  3. Non-Compliant
  4. Not Applicable
- **Mandatory Fields:** Descriptive notes (objective evidence) and attachment upload
