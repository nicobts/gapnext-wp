# Gap Analysis Checklist IATF 16949:2016

> Checklist based on the **IATF 16949:2016** standard (Main Text), specific to the automotive sector.  
> Includes **100 control points** based on Clauses 4–10 of the normative text.

---

## Checklist Methodology

- **Response Options:** Compliant | Partially Compliant | Non-Compliant | Not Applicable
- **Notes Field:** Mandatory (objective evidence)
- **Attachment Field:** Document upload functionality
