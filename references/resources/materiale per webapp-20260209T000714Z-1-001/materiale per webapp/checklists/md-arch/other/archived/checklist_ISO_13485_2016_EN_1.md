# Gap Analysis Checklist UNI CEI EN ISO 13485:2016

> Checklist based on the **ISO 13485:2016** standard (Quality Management Systems for Medical Devices).  
> Includes **100 control points** based on Clauses 4–8.

---

## Checklist Methodology

- **Response Options:**
  1. Compliant
  2. Partially Compliant
  3. Non-Compliant
  4. Not Applicable
- **Notes Field:** Mandatory to describe evidence or reasons for non-applicability
- **Attachments:** Field for uploading supporting files (e.g., procedures, risk plans, validations)
