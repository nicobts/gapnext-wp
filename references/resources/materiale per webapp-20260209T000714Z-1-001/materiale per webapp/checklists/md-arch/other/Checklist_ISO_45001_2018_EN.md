# Gap Analysis Checklist UNI ISO 45001:2018

> Checklist based on the **UNI ISO 45001:2018** standard — Occupational Health and Safety (OH&S) Management Systems.  
> Includes **100 control points** based on normative requirements.

---

## Methodology

- **Consecutive Numbering:** 1–100
- **Response Options:** 1) Compliant, 2) Partially Compliant, 3) Non-Compliant, 4) Not Applicable
- **Mandatory Fields:** Descriptive notes (objective evidence) and attachment upload
